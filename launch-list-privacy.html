Launch list privacy notice

Last updated 25 November 2026

Some of our apps are not in the App Store or Google Play yet. On their websites you can leave your email address to hear when they launch. This notice covers only that launch list. Each app has its own privacy policy for the app itself.

What we keep

  • Your email address and which app you asked about.
  • Whether you also said yes to news about our other apps. This box is never ticked for you.
  • When you signed up, the page you signed up on, and which emails we have sent you.
  • A one-way hash of your network address, used only to stop automated signups. We do not keep the address itself.

What we use it for

  • One welcome email confirming you are on the list.
  • One email when the app launches, with links to the stores.
  • Only if you said yes: occasional emails about other Greatdev Studios apps.

We do not sell or share your address, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The list is stored and the emails are sent using Cloudflare (database and email service), which processes the data for us.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after the app launches if you only asked about that app, whichever comes first. After you unsubscribe we keep only what we need to make sure we do not email you again.

Unsubscribe or delete

Every email has an unsubscribe link, which works straight away. To have your address deleted entirely, or for any question, email greate43.sk.dev@gmail.com.

Greatdev Studios is a sole trader based in Australia.
